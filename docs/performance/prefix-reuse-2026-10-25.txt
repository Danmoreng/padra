Q8/Q8_0 prefix reuse qualification — 2026-10-25

One encoded snapshot retains the 21 local rings; unchanged global prefix rows stay in place.
Codes and scale bits are copied directly without requantization or a BF16 cache shadow.
MTP also retains its draft BF16 ring and the preceding target hidden row.
HTTP Chat sessions and Responses continuations reuse the matching resident session.
Another GPU owner or invalid/stale prefix causes a full replay; this is a single-session GPU cache.

Restore preserves the original chunk grid. Capture moves an anchor back by up to three tokens
if necessary to leave four rows in the first partial chunk. This avoids an EXL3 T<4 arithmetic
change solely due to prefix reuse. The ledger binds runtime, generation, capacity, format and tokens.
